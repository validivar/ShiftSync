What is ShiftSync?

From hospitals and military units to retail chains and NGOs, ShiftSync handles complex workforce scheduling with precision and fairness.

Supported Shift Patterns

ShiftSync supports more shift patterns than any other free scheduling tool. Each is mathematically computed to ensure fairness, compliance, and adequate rest:

4x4 Schedule

Morning/Afternoon/Night/Off rotation. 8-hour shifts. Perfect for 24/7 operations with balanced coverage.

Panama Plan (2-2-3)

12-hour shifts: 2 on, 2 off, 3 on, 2 off. Popular in emergency services, oil & gas, and security.

Dupont Shift

Complex 28-day pattern: 4 nights, 3 off, 3 days, 1 off, 3 nights, 3 off, 4 days, 7 off. Manufacturing favourite.

Pitman Schedule

4 on, 4 off with 12-hour shifts. Long recovery periods. Common in fire brigades and police services.

DDNNOO Shift

2 day, 2 night, 2 rest day cycle. NHS and healthcare standard rotation.

Mixed 8+12hr

60% standard 8-hour + 40% extended 12-hour night duties. Includes 2N2O through 7N7O patterns for flexible healthcare scheduling.
