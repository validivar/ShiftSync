Frequently Asked Questions

Is ShiftSync really 100% free?
Yes — ShiftSync is completely free forever with no registration required. Core scheduling features will always remain free. We sustain operations through ethical advertising, voluntary donations, and affiliate partnerships.
What shift patterns does ShiftSync support?
ShiftSync supports 20+ patterns: Panama Plan (2-2-3), 4x4, Dupont (28-day), Pitman (4 on/4 off), DDNNOO, 4-10, 8-hour standard, 12-hour, 24-hour call duty, 48-hour weekend call, Mixed 8+12hr, and 60/72/80-hour schedules.
How is ShiftSync better than Deputy, Shyftplan, or Agendrix?
ShiftSync is completely free (Deputy costs $4.50+/user/month, Shyftplan €3+/user). We offer more built-in scheduling algorithms, require no registration, work fully offline, and export to PDF and Excel for free. We focus on mathematical scheduling precision while paid tools focus on subscription upsells.
Does ShiftSync store my data on a server?
No. All your data — staff members, rosters, settings — is stored locally in your browser's localStorage. Nothing is transmitted to our servers. Your workforce data remains completely private, and the app works fully offline.
Can I export the roster to PDF or Excel?
Yes! After generating a roster, use the Export PDF, Export Excel, or Print buttons. Both formats are fully supported using jsPDF and SheetJS libraries — completely free with no watermarks.
Does ShiftSync work on mobile and tablets?
Yes. ShiftSync is fully responsive and works on smartphones and tablets. For generating large rosters with many staff members, a desktop or wide-screen tablet is recommended for the best experience.
Can I import staff from a CSV file?
Yes! Click the "Import CSV" button in Staff Management. Format: FirstName, LastName, Rank, EmploymentType, Skills (semicolon-separated). A header row is automatically detected and skipped if present.
What languages does ShiftSync support?
ShiftSync supports 50+ languages via Google Translate. Use the language selector in the top navigation bar. The interface auto-detects your browser language. Manual language selection is also available for all users worldwide.
How does leave management work?
In the "Staff Availability & Leave" section, select a staff member, choose the leave type (Sick, Annual, Maternity, Study, etc.), set the start and end dates, and click "Add Leave". When you generate the roster, those dates will automatically display the leave type code.
What is fatigue management and why does it matter?
Fatigue management limits the maximum number of consecutive shifts a staff member can work and enforces minimum rest hours between shifts (e.g., the EU Working Time Directive requires 11 hours between shifts). Set these values in the Planning Period section. This protects staff wellbeing and reduces scheduling errors.
Is ShiftSync suitable for hospitals and healthcare facilities?
Yes — ShiftSync was designed with healthcare scheduling in mind. It supports ICU staffing patterns, call duty rotation, leave management, compliance tracking, and multi-cadre rosters. Always verify final schedules with your compliance officer before implementation.
